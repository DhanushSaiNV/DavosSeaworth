## 1. Summary

Every signal the accountability chain consumes has been computed and thrown away since FP-02: the per-turn extraction emits `intent_flags` (`give_up`, `false_constraint`, `concession`, `quit_commitment`, `admission`) and `openness_delta` on every chat turn, the journal chain emits `openness_delta` + `admission_flag` every evening, and the classifier has emitted `quit_signal` since FP-03 — all logged, none applied. Every table the chain writes has stood empty since `0007`: `habits` (the D24 lifecycle), `trust_events` (the D20 audit log), `profiles.trust_score`/`dialog_state`/`lockdown_started_at`, and the quest columns on `metrics` (`is_quest`, `quest_status`, `quest_deadline`). Four of the five unimplemented §2.3 job types are this item's: `drift_scan`, `quest_expire`, `strike_fire`, `histogram_daily`. PRD §3.1's Side Quests, §3.2's Strike and impulse interception, §3.3's Anti-Quit Lockdown and Oath, and §4.5's "Davos secures an admission… he forces the user to write their Oath" have nowhere to land.

FP-08 builds all five links as wiring over standing machinery. **Drift/quests:** a `drift_scan` job (FAST, one scan per 10-min tick, the watchdog shape) evaluating the four §7.3 patterns per user — late starts, compliance drop, energy slide, impulse cluster — inserting reason-tagged `intervention` jobs whose handler generates the confrontation and, for the soft patterns, runs FP-07's `proposeMetric` in a new quest mode; on acceptance a new `accept-quest` function creates the quest metric through `insertValidatedMetric` (gate-before-persist, D10's letter), the dashboard renders it as a `ProgressBar` with zero FP-07 changes, and `quest_expire` archives it with a chat debrief at deadline. **Habits/Strike:** drift surfaces habits (impulse ≥ 3/week or ≥ 3 journal `habit_flag`s — one new column, the only schema FP-08 adds), confronts, and a concession-without-commitment turn starts the watch; `strike_fire` generates the Tiered Proof Strike at day X 18:00 local under D11's evidence standard, with a deterministic post-generation check that rejects memory-fact-absent numeric claims. **Trust/Oath:** the D20 rider finally applies the discarded signals — `trust_score` accumulation, `trust_events` audit, streak +2, persona-override and auto-adopt `band_shift` writes, the +1 candor credit for self-logged impulse failures — and the admission-and-threshold trigger demands the Oath in-thread; the next user message is captured verbatim as the non-purgeable `fact_type='oath'` fact (FP-06's guard becomes load-bearing), re-presented at breakdowns. **Lockdown:** give-up signals enter `dialog_state='LOCKDOWN'`, stages 4–5 force the confrontation persona with accountability-filtered rule retrieval and redirect-not-refuse, and three exits (concession, 24 h circuit breaker, S1/S2 severity override) leave it. **Interception:** a `log-impulse` function behind a persistent Android quick-action and an in-app button writes `impulse_failure` rows; `histogram_daily` rebuilds the 14-day urge histogram at 01:00 local and schedules deterministic preemptive pushes at bucket-start − `preemptive_lead_minutes`, each carrying a rotating item from `high_dopamine_activities`.

Cost posture: **four job types implemented, registry unchanged at 15** (`proactive` stays item 10's — the Proactive Conversationalist's profile-gap filling is onboarding's), **one new column** (the first schema growth since `0007`'s feature tables — everything else consumed already stands; migration `0032`), **two new zero-provider sync functions** (`log-impulse`, `accept-quest`), **zero new per-turn provider calls** (the rider reads results stage 1 already computed; the breakdown trigger reads stage 2's), one generation per confrontation/debrief/oath/breakdown plus FP-07's bounded three per quest proposal and one bounded retry per Strike claim-check failure, zero generates anywhere in the drift scan, streak, histogram, or log paths, scheduled inserts ≈ 441/day vs the ~500 §2.3 line (watchdog 288 + drift 144 + metric_eval ~4 + histogram ~5), client logging strictly complies with AGENTS.md §7.8 (`logger` from `lib/logger`, `traceNetworkCall` from `lib/observability`, zero raw `console.*`), and the iOS push ACs bake the standing §13-item-7 fallback (in-thread on next open — the FP-04/FP-05 amendments' pattern).

## 3. User Stories

1. **As a user drifting without noticing**, Davos names the pattern with my own logged data — three late starts, a softening check-in streak — and hands me a deadline-bound Side Quest that shows up on my dashboard the moment I accept it, so the correction is a concrete challenge instead of a vague warning (PRD §3.1).
2. **As a user who keeps refusing to quit a bad habit**, the silence lasts exactly X days and ends in a Strike built only from what I actually did and actually said — logged ground truth first, my own verified words second, and honest probabilistic framing when I never committed to anything — never a fabricated constraint I can dismiss (PRD §3.2).
3. **As a user who keeps showing up**, my candor accumulates invisibly — admissions, streaks, even honestly logging my own failures — until a breakthrough earns Davos's demand for my Oath; and on the day I break down, he answers with my own words beside the numbers, not a lecture (PRD §3.3, §4.5).
4. **As a user fighting doomscroll urges**, logging a lost battle is one tap from the notification I was about to open anyway — and after a few honest logs, Davos starts pinging me fifteen minutes before my danger hour with the alternative I myself chose (PRD §3.2).
5. **As the beta operator** (solo, $0), four job types go live with zero new tables (one column), every trigger is deterministic with the LLM confined to message composition behind those gates, a Strike near a crisis defers itself, two kill switches (`drift_scan_enabled`, `strike_fire_enabled`) stop the heaviest loops cold, and the whole chain's per-day cost is a handful of bounded generates inside the §12 envelope.
